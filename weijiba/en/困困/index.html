Kunkun
Deer Group members
NameZhan Shaoyuan

Kunkun is the nickname Zhan Shaoyuan uses in the Deer Group. The chat archive also records the names Coach Kun and King Kunkun.[1]

Names

Between 14:51:16 and 14:51:26 on 5 June 2025, five messages asked who Kunkun was, several of them from Deng Yuhang. Zhan called himself Kunkun on 23 March 2026.[1]

Chat expressions

On 17 March and 3 August 2026, Zhan wrote, respectively, “You, Wei, study it frame by frame” and “Let's study it frame by frame together, 🦌 group.” On 30–31 July, he used “align on the level of detail” to discuss how much detail a conversation required.[1]

Coverage in 603 Vision

In September 2025, 603 Vision reported that Zhan presented ideas for drone applications after a recruitment talk.[2] A March 2026 article described how he found a phone and took it to a police station.[3]
