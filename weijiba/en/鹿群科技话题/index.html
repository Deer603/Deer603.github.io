Technology discussions in the Deer Group cover hardware and digital devices, engineering projects, digital platforms, AI, education, software development and data security. A study of the group's technology and IT discussions classified 1,721 messages under these headings.[1]

Engineering projects and coursework

603 Vision's report on a September 2025 recruitment event listed drone applications, an ultrasonic directional speaker, a grow light and a tea maker. Another article, published in October 2025, discussed Peltier cooling, coatings, vertical greenery and lifestyle changes as possible responses to hot weather.[2][3]

On 2 March 2026, Wei Ziqi shared course notes on introductory robotics. A discussion of robot kinematics on 23 June covered the definition of the Jacobian matrix and how to identify singularities.[4]

Devices and software

On 6 August 2026, Luo Yulun and Wei Ziqi discussed laptop ports, USB-C power and the weight of power adapters. Members shared the 603AI and KunCode project pages on 12 October. Links to the DeerPhotos repository and release page appeared on 27 October.[4]
