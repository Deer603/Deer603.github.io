Chronology of the Deer Group
Deer Group chronology
Period covered12 May 2025 to 26 November 2026
Basis for datesMessage timestamps

The chronology of the Deer Group draws on its chat archive to document selected campus conversations, group expressions and links to public projects. The available records cover 12 May 2025 through 26 November 2026.[1]

2025

  • 12 May: earliest surviving message in the chat archive.[1]
  • 5 June: several messages asked about the nickname “Kunkun.”[1]
  • 23 October: members shared three versions of an image prompt using Rembrandt's The Night Watch as a compositional reference for a group portrait.[1]

January–August 2026

  • 13 January: Peng Peng posted the evening reminder “Time to check in, kids” and repeated it on several later dates.[1]
  • 2 March: Wei Ziqi shared notes for an introductory robotics course.[1]
  • 23 June: members discussed the Jacobian matrix and singularities in robot kinematics.[1]
  • 10 July and 7 August: the expressions “collective high cognition” and “commun🦌ism,” respectively, appeared in the chat.[1]

September–November 2026

  • 25 September: a member shared a link to the Deer603 website.[1]
  • 12 October: members shared the project pages for 603AI and KunCode.[1]
  • 16 October: a link to the You're a Deer website appeared during a discussion of the Deer Test.[1]
  • 27 October: a link to the DeerPhotos repository appeared at 18:07, followed by its 3.0.0 release page at 19:02.[1][2]
