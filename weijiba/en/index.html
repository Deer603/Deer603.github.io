    Featured article

    Frog's Corner is a photography website set in a six-person dormitory. Visitors can move between bunks and enter Frog’s room to open The Light Archive, a photo album published by Wei Ziqi under the name KEMA.

    The album’s twelve photographs fall into four chapters: historic architecture, double exposures, street photography and sculpture exhibitions. The first chapter shows temple halls, bamboo paths and stone lions at Xiangji Temple in Xi’an. In the second, dark and light versions of a double exposure pair the stone lions with calligraphy.
