Site directory/xiaweicheng/

Xia Weicheng's Portfolio showcases Xia's engineering projects and photography. Hosted alongside 603web, its main page features travel photographs and accounts of campus life. Three separate pages describe a robot power controller, a smart tea maker and a photography assistant.[6] Luo Yulun shared the site in the Deer Group chat on 15 October and 10 November 2026.[1][2]

Hardware projects

The RoboMaster case study examines the power supply of an infantry robot and explains how a power control board and a supercapacitor respond to changing loads. Photographs show the assembled prototype, while a 3D rendering of the board shows the component layout.[3]

The smart tea maker was designed to have tea ready at a suitable drinking temperature when the user arrived home. The case study shows a circuit schematic, a board layout and photographs of the prototype before and after power-up. Its working display shows the water level and temperature. Concept drawings depict the proposed appliance enclosure and mobile interface.[4]

LensPilot AI photography assistant

The LensPilot AI case study describes a tool for beginning photographers reviewing their shots. According to the project page, the working web prototype accepts photo uploads, generates thumbnails locally and reads EXIF camera settings. A personal photography skills profile and a seven-day learning plan are proposed features illustrated in interface mockups.[5]
