Li Jianyu

Article
Read

Weiji Encyclopedia, Kunkun's Encyclopedia

Share article

Li Jianyu is a Deer Group member. According to 603 Vision, he played bass at a Desk Park rehearsal and took part in a demonstration of a smart plant grow light.[1][2]

Bass guitar

A 603 Vision profile published on 10 November 2025 described Li practicing bass.[3] The account's band report later that month lists him as the bassist at Desk Park's first rehearsal.[1]

Plant grow light

According to 603 Vision's September 2025 report on a recruitment event, Li Jianyu and Li Yanzhen jointly demonstrated a smart plant grow light.[2]
