603web is an interactive website set in Room 603. Its main page, “603, Our Home,” lets visitors read roommate profiles, post on a simulated dormitory forum, shop for items and follow clues in room-inspection reports. On 25 September 2026, Luo Yulun shared the main page in the Deer Group chat.[1][2]

Forum, shop and room inspections

The forum charges eight “attention points” per new post. The shop uses a separate virtual currency, Deer Coins. Visitors can collect a daily allowance of 100 Deer Coins. The shop tracks purchases and applies limits separately for each item.[1][3]

The room-inspection reports turn roll call into a puzzle. Viewing the files of absent residents, reading the inspection log, providing a clue and closing the bounty each account for one quarter of the puzzle. Completing all four reveals a diagram of the relationships among the people involved.[3]

Saving progress

New posts and replies are stored in the author's browser. The site uses the browser's localStorage to retain posts, points, Deer Coins, purchases and puzzle progress between visits. A reset control clears the saved game.[3]
