Xia Weicheng

Xia Weicheng is a Deer Group member. 603 Vision reported on his smart tea maker and featured him in travel writing, posters and an essay on campus life.[1][2][3][5]

Engineering projects and discussion

603 Vision featured Xia's smart tea maker in its September 2025 report on a recruitment event.[1] On 23 June 2026, he asked about the meaning of the Jacobian matrix during a group discussion of robot kinematics.[4]

Appearances in 603 Vision

In December 2025, 603 Vision published a campus essay about Xia's name, “Three Characters Etched into the College.”[5] Its March 2026 travelogue “At Yueyang Tower, Looking Toward Zhejiang” lists him among the travelers.[2] “Xia Weicheng's Journey in Search of His Roots,” a travel poster published on 4 July, traces a route from Changsha to Xiangxi.[3]
