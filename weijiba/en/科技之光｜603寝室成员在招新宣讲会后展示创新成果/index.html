“The Light of Technology: Room 603 Residents Present Their Projects After a Recruitment Talk” is a 603 Vision report published on 16 September 2025. It describes six members presenting their engineering projects after a recruitment talk.[1]

Projects covered

According to the article, Zhan Shaoyuan presented a proposal for drone applications, while Luo Yulun and Wei Ziqi described an ultrasonic directional speaker. Li Jianyu and Li Yanzhen demonstrated a smart grow light, and Xia Weicheng presented a smart tea maker.[1]
