Deer Group
WeChat group
Group name in the November 2026 exportZetacognition🦌
Earliest surviving record12 May 2025
Principal topicsCampus life, technology and entertainment

The Deer Group is a WeChat group whose members know one another mainly through campus life. Its discussions cover coursework, engineering, digital devices, entertainment and everyday life. Expressions used within the group include “high cognition” and “commun🦌ism.”[1][2] The chat export of 26 November 2026 records the group's name as “Zetacognition🦌.”[2]

Topics of discussion

An early survey identified campus affairs, technology, entertainment and interactions among members as frequent topics. Members discussed assignments, exams and introductory robotics, as well as equipment selection, division of project work and software tools.[1]

The archive also records members sharing projects such as 603AI, KunCode and DeerPhotos. Group expressions appear in reminders, comments and mock slogans.[2]

Forms of address

Members sometimes call one another “鹿友” or “🦌友” (“deer friends”). On 16 May 2026, a member asked, “What's for lunch, deer friends?” A Dragon Boat Festival greeting likewise addressed “deer friends.” The plural addresses the group, while the singular can refer to one member, as in a 28 June message about a deer friend who had run into Kunkun.[3]

Archives and statistics

A survey covering the period through 25 August 2026 counted 57,216 messages across 471 calendar days, with messages on 469 days. Fourteen people had sent at least 100 messages and qualified for its analysis of core participants.[1]

The 26 November 2026 export runs from 23:31:53 on 12 May 2025 to 13:59:36 on 26 November 2026. It contains 49,393 messages spread over 557 days, including text, replies quoting other messages, system messages, links, forwarded chats, transfers and red packets.[2]
