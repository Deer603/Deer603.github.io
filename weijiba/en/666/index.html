666
Deer Group expression
Archive matches150 standalone messages
Scope of countText messages and quoted replies in the 26 November 2026 export

In the Deer Group, “666” often appears on its own after someone shares something. It can be a quick comment, an acknowledgment or an expression of admiration. Its tone depends on the subject and surrounding messages.[1]

Usage in the group

On 2 September 2025, a member suggested taking a table from the project space back to the dormitory. Another replied “666.” On 13 October, “666” appeared between two messages about scores.[1]

In text messages and quoted replies in the 26 November 2026 archive, 150 message bodies consisted of exactly “666” after leading and trailing whitespace was removed.[1]
